Why we built this

We kept hearing the same question from parents.

“Is my kid going to make it in whatever this economy turns into?”
“I caught him using ChatGPT on his essay. Do I ban it, or is that a mistake too?”

The biggest bet we're both making right now is on AI, and the honest, difficult answer is that 13 to 15 is exactly when the habit sets. Right now it points at dodging homework. So we thought: catch it here, while they're old enough to be mentored like adults and young enough that nothing is riding on it yet. Worst case, they finish something real and reach applications with years of shipped work behind them. Best case, they grow up fluent in the biggest gold rush in history: AI.

Either way, the habit points at building. That is the bet we built this on.
