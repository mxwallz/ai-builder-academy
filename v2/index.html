Questions and answers.

Is this instead of college prep?

No, it is complementary. The build is the passion project consultants charge five figures to invent, except your teen actually makes it. Whatever happens to college, they walk into applications holding the real thing.
My teen already uses ChatGPT for everything. Isn't that the problem?

That habit is the raw material. Right now it points at avoiding work. Twelve weeks from now it points at producing it, and they can explain every decision in the build because the decisions were theirs.
Can't they just learn this free online?

A rare, self-driven teenager will. For everyone else, and you already know which one yours is on a Tuesday night, the finishing is the hard part. Structure, a live mentor, and a real deadline are what turn good intentions into a running business.
Will they actually stick with it for 12 weeks?

They pick the project, so it is theirs. Then the structure holds: a small cohort that notices when they are gone, a mentor who knows their name, and a launch date on the calendar.
They're 17. Is it too late to start?

A senior who launches a real business this fall walks into applications and interviews holding proof most college graduates cannot show. It is the fastest age to see results, not the latest.
What does it cost?

The first call is free. If your teen is a fit, we quote the 12-week cohort on that call, once we know what they would be building.
