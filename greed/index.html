The Operator

This is the teenager who cannot leave a broken process alone. Give them and within a month there is a system behind it that runs better than the adults' version. They are not short on drive, they are short on a problem big enough to deserve it.

What Operators ship in 12 weeks

The booking and follow-up system, the kind that recovered twelve hours a week for a family business. Real customers, real money, running without them.

Where Operators stall

They polish instead of launching. There is always one more feature. Our mentors force the ship date and hold it.

Operators are a fit for roughly 2 of the 6 seats in each cohort. A discovery call is where we confirm it and scope their first venture.

The Inventor

Ideas arrive faster than they can be finished. Point this teen at and they will have three prototypes and no launch. The talent is obvious. The missing piece is somebody who makes them pick one and put it in front of strangers.

What Inventors ship in 12 weeks

A product other people actually use. The study assistant 300 classmates were on before finals started as exactly this archetype.

Where Inventors stall

Week five, when the new idea looks better than the current one. We make them finish the first one anyway.

Inventors are a fit for roughly 2 of the 6 seats in each cohort. A discovery call is where we confirm it and scope their first venture.

The Advocate

Nothing this teen does stays personal for long. Interest in turns into a group, a cause, or a campaign to get everyone else involved. They already have the hardest thing to teach, which is caring out loud. What they lack is a tool that makes the caring scale.

What Advocates ship in 12 weeks

Something live with a real organization. The volunteer matching app running for a community group is the shape this takes.

Where Advocates stall

They wait for permission from the organization. We teach them to build the thing first and bring it to the table finished.

Advocates are a fit for roughly 1 of the 6 seats in each cohort. A discovery call is where we confirm it and scope their first venture.

The Analyst

This teen reads the primary source. A passing interest in becomes a spreadsheet, a ranking, and a strong opinion they will defend against an adult. Depth was never their problem; shipping before they feel finished is, so that is what we train.

What Analysts ship in 12 weeks

An AI agent or research tool that answers a question nobody else bothered to answer properly, published where the field can see it.

Where Analysts stall

Research becomes the hiding place. We cap the reading and move them into build week whether they feel ready or not.

Analysts are a fit for roughly 1 of the 6 seats in each cohort. A discovery call is where we confirm it and scope their first venture.

Questions and answers.

Is this instead of college prep?

No, it is complementary. The build is the passion project consultants charge five figures to invent, except your teen actually makes it. Whatever happens to college, they walk into applications holding the real thing.
My teen already uses ChatGPT for everything. Isn't that the problem?

That habit is the raw material. Right now it points at avoiding work. Twelve weeks from now it points at producing it, and they can explain every decision in the build because the decisions were theirs.
Can't they just learn this free online?

A rare, self-driven teenager will. For everyone else, and you already know which one yours is on a Tuesday night, the finishing is the hard part. Structure, a live mentor, and a real deadline are what turn good intentions into a running venture.
Will they actually stick with it for 12 weeks?

They pick the venture, so it is theirs. Then the structure holds: a small cohort that notices when they are gone, a mentor who knows their name, and a launch date on the calendar.
They're 17. Is it too late to start?

A senior who ships a real venture this fall walks into applications and interviews holding proof most college graduates cannot show. It is the fastest age to see results. The build lands while every door is still open.
